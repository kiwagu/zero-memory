you may do with your own work elsewhere.

You accept this agreement by stating so on your first pull request, in the
comment the CLA bot asks for:

The signature is recorded against your GitHub account and covers every
contribution you make to the Project from then on.

private thread through the repository's **Security** tab (_Report a
vulnerability_, marked as a CLA question) so an entity agreement can be
arranged.
