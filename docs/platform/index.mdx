How the zero-memory platform is built and operated.

[Retrieval](/docs/platform/retrieval) is the hybrid RAG pipeline behind every
recall: two search legs, rank fusion, chunk-level embeddings, and the signals
that reorder the result. The dashboard is composed from one component system,
described in [UI stack](/docs/platform/ui-stack). The e2e harness that
exercises it, and the testing contract that goes with it, are in
[End-to-end testing](/docs/platform/e2e-testing).

A running instance reports on itself in three places.
[Observability](/docs/platform/observability) covers logging, request
tracing, and diagnostics. [Usage events](/docs/platform/usage-events) is the
append-only record of metered work, and the
[audit log](/docs/platform/audit-log) records who did what, in the database.

What external clients depend on is written down twice.
[Contracts and errors](/docs/platform/contracts-and-errors) describes the
schema-first contracts, the versioning, and the error taxonomy;
[Deprecations](/docs/DEPRECATIONS) is the ledger of deprecated surfaces and
breaking changes in the client contract.

The last three pages are about whether memory is actually helping.
[Value metering](/docs/platform/value-metering) is the instrumentation that
measures what memory contributes,
[judge precision](/docs/platform/judge-precision) is how automated judgments
are kept trustworthy, and the
[memory benchmark](/docs/platform/roi-benchmark) measures what you would lose
without it.
